01 — Flight Record

It didn't go to space
because it was famous.
It became famous because
it went to space.

NASA didn't accept sponsorships or endorsements. Every chronograph submitted for qualification was bought off the shelf, stripped of its brand, and run through tests designed to break it. The Speedmaster was the only one that didn't.

1962
First Omega in space
Astronaut Wally Schirra wears his personal Speedmaster aboard Mercury-Atlas 8 — the watch reaches orbit before NASA has even chosen an official timepiece.
1965
NASA flight-qualified
After tests spanning extreme heat, vacuum, humidity, shock and vibration, the Speedmaster is the only chronograph to pass every stage and is qualified for all manned space missions.
1969
Worn on the lunar surface
During Apollo 11, Buzz Aldrin wears his Speedmaster on the Moon's surface — the first watch worn there, while Neil Armstrong's remains inside the Lunar Module as backup timing.
1970
Timed the crew home
Aboard the crippled Apollo 13, with onboard electronics powered down, the crew uses a Speedmaster to hand-time a critical 14-second engine burn that corrects their course back to Earth.

02 — Instrument, Not Accessory

Three sub-dials.
One decision made
for a reason.

The tri-compax layout packs a 30-minute counter, small seconds, and a 12-hour counter around the chronograph centre, with a tachymeter scale on the bezel for reading speed over a known distance. The dome above it is hesalite, not sapphire — a deliberate trade-off, because acrylic doesn't shatter into sharp fragments in a pressurised cabin. On the tension ring, a small orange dot at ninety gave astronauts a fixed point to eyeball a countdown against.

03 — Manually Wound, Deliberately

Nothing in it depends
on gravity to work.

A hand-wound movement has no rotor bearing that needs weight to swing, and no battery to fail in a vacuum — which is exactly why it was still the right choice decades after automatic movements became common. The modern calibre 3861 keeps that manual-wind column-wheel chronograph architecture, adds a co-axial escapement, and is Master Chronometer certified — tested for resistance to magnetic fields up to 15,000 gauss.
